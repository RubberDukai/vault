---
title: Flatbread
summary: Flour, water, salt, a hot pan. Bread in ten minutes anywhere there is heat.
serves: 8 breads
time: 20 minutes
---

The oldest bread and the most forgiving. No oven, no yeast, no waiting.

## You need

- 300 g flour, any kind
- ½ teaspoon salt
- About 180 ml warm water
- A spoon of oil or fat if you have it (not essential)

## Method

1. Mix to a dough, knead two or three minutes until smooth, rest ten minutes under a cloth if you can.
2. Divide into eight. Roll or press each thin — 2–3 mm — on a floured surface.
3. Cook on a dry pan, griddle or flat stone over a good heat, about a minute a side, until blistered and spotted brown. Press with a cloth to encourage it to puff.
4. Stack under a cloth to keep soft.

Wrap anything in it. A little yoghurt or mashed potato in the dough makes it softer; a spoon of baking powder makes it thicker and puffier.
