---
title: Porridge and cooked grains
summary: How much water, how long, for every grain you might have a sack of.
serves: varies
time: 15–60 minutes
---

Grain and water is the meal that has fed more people than any other. The ratios below are by volume: one cup of grain to the given cups of water. Salt at the end. Add anything you have on top.

| Grain | Water | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rolled oats | 2 (water or milk) | 5 min | Stir. Pinhead oats: 3 water, 25 min |
| White rice | 1.5 | 12 min, lid on, then rest 10 | Do not stir, do not lift the lid |
| Brown rice | 2.5 | 40 min | Soak an hour first to shorten |
| Pearl barley | 3 | 40 min | Chewy; good in soup |
| Bulgur | 2 | 12 min, off the heat | Pour boiling water over, cover |
| Couscous | 1 | 5 min, off the heat | Boiling water, cover, fluff |
| Polenta / cornmeal | 4 | 30–40 min, stirring | Sets solid when cool; slice and fry |
| Lentils (red) | 3 | 20 min | Collapse into a thick soup |
| Lentils (green/brown) | 3 | 30 min | Hold their shape |
| Dried beans | cover well | Soak overnight, then 1–2 hours | **Boil hard for 10 minutes first** — raw kidney beans are poisonous |
| Chickpeas | cover well | Soak overnight, then 1.5 hours | Pinch of bicarbonate softens them |

A **pressure cooker** cuts the long ones to a quarter of the time and a quarter of the fuel. A **hay box** (bring to the boil, then bury the pot in a box of hay or blankets for hours) finishes beans, barley and stews with no fuel at all.
