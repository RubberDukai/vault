---
title: Soda bread
summary: A loaf in under an hour with no yeast, no proving, no kneading.
serves: 1 loaf
time: 50 minutes
---

The bread to make when there is no yeast and no time. Bicarbonate of soda and something acid — buttermilk, soured milk, yoghurt thinned with water, or milk with a spoon of vinegar — do the rising in the oven.

## You need

- 450 g flour (plain, wholemeal, or half and half — anything that is not self-raising)
- 1 level teaspoon bicarbonate of soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- About 400 ml buttermilk or soured milk (milk plus 1 tablespoon vinegar or lemon juice, left five minutes)

## Method

1. Heat the oven as hot as it goes — 220 °C, or a wood oven in which a scrap of paper browns in a few seconds.
2. Mix the dry things. Make a well, pour in most of the liquid, and bring together with a knife then your hand into a soft, shaggy dough. Add the rest of the liquid if it is dry. Do not knead — the less you handle it the lighter it is.
3. Shape into a round on a floured tray, about 4 cm thick. Cut a deep cross across the top, nearly to the base.
4. Bake 30–35 minutes. It is done when the base sounds hollow when knocked.
5. Cool wrapped in a cloth for a soft crust, or bare for a hard one. Best the day it is made; toast it after that.

**In a pot over a fire:** put the dough in a greased lidded iron pot, lid on, in the embers with more embers on the lid. 35–45 minutes; check at 30.
