# Credits & licences

The Vault is two things with two different owners. The **program** — the server, the reader, the map engine, the games — is original work. The **knowledge** — the encyclopedias, the textbooks, the maps, the books — was made by other people and is included here under the open licences that permit it. Those licences ask for two things in return: that the makers are credited, and that the terms travel with the copy. This page is that credit, and it travels with every copy of the Vault.

## The program

The Vault's own code is © 2026 Joseph Watkins and released under the **MIT Licence** — free to use, copy, change and redistribute, with no warranty. The written **handbook, lessons and language courses** are also original work and are the author's copyright; you may use and share them within a copy of the Vault. None of this relicenses the third-party knowledge below, which keeps its own terms.

The Vault bundles the **Node.js** runtime so it can run on a machine with nothing installed. Node.js is distributed under the MIT Licence and includes components (OpenSSL and others) under their own licences; their notices ship with the runtime.

## Not included in a sold or shared copy

Some sources are offered for personal download but carry a **NonCommercial** licence, so they are never bundled into a copy that is sold or handed on:

- **iFixit** repair guides — CC BY-NC-SA.
- **Sentinel-2 cloudless** satellite imagery (EOX IT Services GmbH, modified Copernicus Sentinel data) — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- **Hesperian** health guides (e.g. *Where There Is No Doctor*) — used only with permission.

## A note on the maps you see

The map credits its sources on the map itself, in the corner, for whichever base map and overlays you have switched on — because that is what the map licences ask for, and because it is honest about where the ground under your feet was drawn.
